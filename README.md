# CEO Learnings

A growing library of what operators actually said, read three ways:

1. **Sources** - the unadulterated record: direct links to the original videos and interviews. Captured transcripts are kept private, off this repo.
2. **People** - what each person's lesson adds up to: summary, context, sources.
3. **Themes** - what the library says about each question, every position with its author's name attached.

Live at https://robertmuse6.github.io/ceo-learnings/

## How it is organised

- `data.js` is the whole library. One record per learning, tagged with its person, its themes, an exact quote and its source. All three views render from these records in `index.html`, so the views cannot drift apart.
- Adding a source = adding records to `data.js` (source, person if new, any new themes, and the learnings). No page edits needed.
- Public pages carry original summaries with limited attributed excerpts. Full third-party transcripts and personal reflections are deliberately kept out of this repo and its history.

## Source fidelity

- The original video or interview is the authority; auto-transcripts are working copies and may contain errors.
- Where two people disagree on a theme, both positions stay visible with names. No merged consensus.
- Interpretation is labeled as interpretation.
